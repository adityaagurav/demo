Few facts about the world we live in

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet that supports life. About 71% of its surface is covered by water, with the rest being land. It has a protective atmosphere rich in nitrogen and oxygen, which helps regulate temperature and supports living organisms. Earth rotates once every 24 hours (day) and revolves around the Sun in about 365 days (year).
